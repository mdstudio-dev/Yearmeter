Privacy Policy — Yearmeter
Developer: Ouayat Studio

Contact: ouayatstudio@gmail.com

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the Yearmeter app ("the app") handles and why. The app is intended for adults (18 years and older).

1. Data the app itself stores
The countdowns and "days since" counters you create (name, date, color, options) and your settings (widget style, theme, first day of the week) are stored only on your device. Ouayat Studio has no server for this app and never receives this information. If you turned on your phone's backup, this data may be included in your own device backup, under the terms of your backup provider.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, location or any other personal information, and it uses no sensitive permission.

2. Sharing your year progress
When you tap Share year progress, the app draws an image of your year progress on your device and saves it in the app's private temporary storage. The image is passed only to the app you choose in your phone's share menu, with a temporary permission to read that one image. The image contains no personal information, and Ouayat Studio never receives it. What happens to the image after you share it is governed by the app you shared it with.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
your IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate location,
information about how you interact with ads (views, taps),
diagnostic and performance information about the ad SDK,
general device information (model, operating system version, language).
Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. It is encrypted in transit. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states)
On first launch, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it. Ads are requested only after your choice allows it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options (ads consent), shown when it applies to your region. If you refuse, the app keeps working fully; you may see no ads or non-personalized ads.

5. Advertising ID: reset or opt out
You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact path depends on your device). You can also manage ad personalization at https://adssettings.google.com

6. Children
The app is not directed at children and is intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Ad requests are marked as not child-directed. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help.

7. Data retention and deletion
Data stored by the app stays on your device until you delete a countdown or counter, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Shared images are kept only in temporary storage, which is replaced each time you share and is removed when you clear the app's cache or uninstall. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can manage it with the controls in sections 4 and 5. For any request, write to ouayatstudio@gmail.com.

8. Security
The app sends no data of its own over the network. The ad and consent SDKs use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and the app blocks unencrypted network traffic.

9. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and other US state laws)
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising. Because Ouayat Studio holds no personal data about you, most requests concern Google's advertising data: use the Privacy options in the app, the advertising ID controls, or Google's tools. You can also contact us at ouayatstudio@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Permissions used
Internet and network state: only so the ad and consent SDKs can work.
Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.
11. Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date, and important changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
